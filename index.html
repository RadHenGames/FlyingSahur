About Flying Sahur

Flying Sahur is a browser-based endless arcade game created by Rad Hen Games. Guide the character through challenging obstacles, react quickly, and survive as long as possible. The game becomes faster over time, encouraging players to improve their timing and reflexes with every attempt.

Designed to load quickly in modern browsers, Flying Sahur offers short, fun gameplay sessions on desktop and mobile devices without requiring any download.

How to Play

  • Press Space, click, or tap to fly.
  • Avoid obstacles.
  • Stay alive to increase your score.
  • Try to beat your personal best.

Features

  • Original gameplay.
  • Endless score chasing.
  • Responsive controls.
  • Desktop and mobile support.
  • Fast browser loading.

Tips

Use gentle movements instead of large jumps and keep your character near the center of the screen to maximize your reaction time.
