Flying Sahur

Fly as far as possible, dodge obstacles, and beat your highest score.

About Flying Sahur

Flying Sahur is an original endless flying game developed by Rad Hen Games. Your objective is to stay in the air for as long as possible while avoiding obstacles that become increasingly difficult as your score grows. The game is designed for quick play sessions and smooth browser performance on desktop and mobile devices.

Each run is different, encouraging players to improve their reaction time and learn the movement mechanics. Whether you are trying to beat your own record or simply enjoy a casual arcade experience, Flying Sahur offers simple controls with progressively challenging gameplay.

How to Play

  • Press Space, click, or tap to fly.
  • Avoid obstacles and stay alive.
  • Survive longer to earn a higher score.
  • Practice your timing to improve each run.

Features

  • Endless arcade gameplay.
  • Fast loading with no installation required.
  • Playable in modern web browsers.
  • Responsive controls for desktop and mobile.
  • Designed and developed by Rad Hen Games.

Tips

Small, controlled movements are usually better than large jumps. Try to stay near the middle of the screen so you have more time to react to upcoming obstacles. Regular practice will help you achieve higher scores.
